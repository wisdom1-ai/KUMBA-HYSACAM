ABOUT HYSACAM

Committed to cleaner and healthier communities.

HYSACAM (Highlands and Western Sanitation Company) is committed to keeping our communities clean and healthy.

Responsible waste management plays an important role in maintaining clean surroundings and supporting healthier communities.

The HYSACAM Kumba Tracker provides a digital platform where residents and staff can access information about waste collection points and community cleaning activities.
