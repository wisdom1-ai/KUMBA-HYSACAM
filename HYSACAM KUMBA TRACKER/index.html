COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are the bin locations real and verified?

No. They are illustrative sample records used to demonstrate the interface. Replace them with verified records before launch.

Does submitting a report alert HYSACAM?

No. In this version, a report is saved in your browser only. A secure backend and an agreed reporting workflow are needed to send reports to an authorized service team.

Can another person see my reports?

Not through this demo website. The reports are stored in local browser storage on this device and browser. Clearing browser data may remove them.

Why does the map need an internet connection?

The map library and map tiles are loaded from online services. If they cannot load, the map may not appear.
