HYSACAM UPDATE: SIGN UP EVERYWHERE, NOTIFICATIONS, INSTANT REPORTS

1. Sign up on every device
- Sign in and Create account pages now have a visible "Sign in | Create account" switch at the top.
- Settings and Profile now show "Create account" and "Sign In" for visitors (before, only Sign In).
- Directory, Cleaning and About show a "Join HYSACAM" card to visitors.
- Phones: shorter brand banner on the auth pages so the form starts higher; email/enter-key hints for Android keyboards.

2. Notifications
- Bell badge now counts UNREAD updates (before it counted pending reports, so it never cleared).
- Tap an update to open its details (status, location, team update, date) and a button to the related page.
- Opening an update marks it read and the badge goes down; "Mark all as read" clears everything.
- Read state is remembered on this device per account. A report status change creates a new unread update.
- Bottom sheet on phones, centred card on tablet and laptop.

3. Reports page speed
- Last known reports are saved on the device and shown instantly; the list refreshes quietly behind the scenes.
- First visit shows placeholders instead of a blank screen; the "Sign in to submit" box no longer flashes for signed-in users.
- Reports/notification requests start before the map and database libraries load (those now load with defer).
- A submitted report appears immediately at the top of the list.
- Server: GET /api/reports now also returns admin_notes so residents can see "Team update".
